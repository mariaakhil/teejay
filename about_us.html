About Us

Who We Are

Teejay Packaging LLC specializes in the manufacturing of high-quality rigid boxes for brands that value precision, durability, and premium presentation. At a time when rigid boxes were largely imported to meet volume and specialty requirements, we identified the need for a fully mechanized, locally based production facility. Our advanced systems ensure consistent craftsmanship, faster turnaround times, and reliable supply for niche and luxury product segments.

Our
Vision

To become the leading rigid box manufacturer in the UAE with a strong presence across GCC, MENA, and European markets, recognized for our craftsmanship, technological excellence, and commitment to environmentally responsible packaging solutions.

Our
Mission

To deliver premium, innovative, and sustainable rigid box packaging solutions that elevate product value and strengthen brand identity. We aim to empower businesses with reliable, locally manufactured packaging that combines precision engineering, creative design, and uncompromising quality.
